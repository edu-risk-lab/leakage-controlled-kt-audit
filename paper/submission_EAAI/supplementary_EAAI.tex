\section{Formal graph-construction objective}
\label{sec:supp-graph-objective}
\noindent This section states the conceptual graph objective referenced in
Section~\ref{sec:formal-problem}. Prerequisite and similarity edges are inferred
in separate greedy pipelines with $\lambda{=}0$ in practice; $\mathrm{sim}$ is
Jaccard or PMI (Section~\ref{sec:graph-sim-theory}).
\begin{equation}
\max_{\Epre^f,\Esim^f}\;
\sum_{(i,j)\in\Epre^f} w_{ij}
\;+\;
\lambda \sum_{(i,j)\in\Esim^f} \mathrm{sim}(c_i,c_j)
\label{eq:graph-objective}
\end{equation}
where $\lambda\ge 0$ would trade prerequisite mass against similarity mass if the
two pipelines were merged (they are not in the released implementation).
\begin{align}
\text{(F1)} \;& \forall e\in\mathcal{E}_f,\;
  S_{\mathrm{hi}}(e)=\emptyset;
  \label{eq:c1}\\
\text{(F2)} \;& \mathcal{U}^{\mathrm{tr}}_f \cap \mathcal{U}^{\mathrm{va}}_f
  = \emptyset,\notag\\
&\; \mathcal{U}^{\mathrm{tr}}_f \cap \mathcal{U}^{\mathrm{te}}_f
  = \emptyset,\notag\\
&\; \mathcal{U}^{\mathrm{va}}_f \cap \mathcal{U}^{\mathrm{te}}_f
  = \emptyset;
  \label{eq:c2}\\
\text{(F3)} \;& \Epre^f \text{ is acyclic after pruning};
  \label{eq:c3}\\
\text{(F4)} \;& |\Epre^f|\le K,\;
  |\mathrm{Out}_{\Epre^f}(i)|\le k;\notag\\
&\; \mathrm{sim}(c_i,c_j)\ge\tau \ \forall (i,j)\in\Esim^f.
  \label{eq:c4}
\end{align}

\section{DDR augmentation operator definitions}
\label{sec:supp-operators}
All operators act on the retained acyclic $\Epre$ at perturbation strength
$p\in\{0.05,0.10,0.20,0.30\}$:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Attribute masking} masks a random subset of edge attributes
  without changing $\Epre$ ($\DDR{=}0$ by construction).
  \item \textbf{Edge dropping} removes each edge independently with probability
  $p$ (expected $\DDR{\approx}p$).
  \item \textbf{Subgraph sampling} performs a random walk until
  $\lceil(1-p)|\mathcal{C}|\rceil$ nodes are visited and returns the induced
  subgraph (boundary edges drop; internal connectivity preserved).
  \item \textbf{Node dropping} removes $\lceil p|\mathcal{C}|\rceil$ nodes and
  all incident edges.
  \item \textbf{\texttt{prereq\_preserve}} draws the same per-edge budget as
  edge dropping but removes only transitively redundant edges, protecting the
  transitive-reduction backbone.
\end{itemize}

\section{$\Delta$AUC intervals for primary pairs}
\label{sec:supp-bootstrap}
Table~S16 (Table~\ref{tab:bootstrap-auc-ci}) reports the mean AUC difference
($\Delta$AUC) between the primary model pairs on XES3G5M under train-only
graph construction. The released bundle reports paired-$t$ 95\% intervals
over three learner-disjoint folds (default; matches fold-level
$\Delta$AUC). Optional row-level learner bootstrap:
\path{scripts/bootstrap_auc_ci.py --learner-bootstrap}.
The GKT deficit under the released 10-epoch budget ($-0.041$; CI $[-0.044,-0.038]$) is more than an order of
magnitude larger than the interval half-width, so the model-specific conclusion
does not hinge on fragile three-fold $t$-tests. Extended-training sensitivity

\section{Controlled Leak Injection}
\label{sec:supp-leak-injection}
Table~S17 (Table~\ref{tab:leak-injection}) probes the sensitivity of the
audit metrics by deliberately injecting $5\%$ and $20\%$ of test-fold
interactions into the train-only prerequisite builder on XES3G5M (fold~0).
Edge counts $|\Epre|$ are \emph{candidate} edges before DAG pruning (the
main text DAG audit reports 701 retained edges after pruning on the clean
fold). Candidate-edge count and $\mathrm{LTES}_f$ increase monotonically with the
injection rate, while $\mathrm{ECR}^{\mathrm{flag}}_f$ remains $0$ (learner-id
sets stay disjoint) and $\mathrm{ECR}^{\mathrm{overlap}}_f$ is already
saturated at $1.0$ on this transition-dense corpus. Flag-level checks alone
therefore miss this contamination channel, supporting the tiered audit design
in the main text.

Table~S18 (Table~\ref{tab:downstream-injection-auc}) reports fold-0 test AUC
after retraining \textit{simpleKT}, GKT, and GIKT on the clean and injected
