EAAI single-column submission package (2026-09-08)
=================================================

Canonical anonymized manuscript: main_EAAI.tex
Bibliography:                   refs_EAAI.bib
Title page (NOT for reviewers): title_page.tex
Highlights (separate upload):   highlights.txt
Cover letter:                   CoverLetter_EAAI.md
Checklist:                      SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md

Compile the anonymized manuscript (this directory):

  pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main_EAAI.tex
  bibtex main_EAAI
  pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main_EAAI.tex
  pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main_EAAI.tex

Compile the supplementary PDF:

  pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode supplementary_EAAI.tex
  bibtex supplementary_EAAI
  pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode supplementary_EAAI.tex
  pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode supplementary_EAAI.tex

Compile the title page and graphical abstract:

  pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode title_page.tex
  pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode graphical_abstract.tex

Main manuscript MUST stay <= 50 pages (EAAI desk-reject rule).
Appendix tables/figures live in supplementary_EAAI.pdf, not in main_EAAI.pdf.

(https://www.elsevier.com/researcher/author/policies-and-guidelines/latex-instructions):

  Manuscript            main_EAAI.pdf
